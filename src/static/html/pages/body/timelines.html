Timelines

A lobby is created against one timeline, and can only draw from decks that belong to it. Eras are ordered, named year-range labels within a timeline (e.g. B.C.E/C.E, or BBY/ABY) used when displaying a card's year — they don't affect gameplay. Categories are each timeline's own list a card is placed into; a deck can only be assigned to a timeline once it has at least one era and at least one category.
